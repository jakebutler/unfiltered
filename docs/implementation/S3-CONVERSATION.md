# S3 natural browser voice conversation

Implementation contract, 2026-10-09. This completes the approved browser voice-chat flow. The current Finish → Review → Send interaction remains a dictation fallback. No code or runtime changes are made here.

## Patient flow

1. After the existing fictional-persona gate, offer **Start voice conversation** alongside typing. Explain once: microphone audio goes to transcription, the clinic does not store raw recordings, recognized words are saved automatically as draft answers, and nothing reaches the care team until summary confirmation. Obtain the fictional-audio consent checkbox and initial start gesture. Reload, leaving or identity loss requires a new start.
2. Keep the question, live words, typing, microphone indicator and **Stop conversation** visible. Use specific states: Connecting microphone; Listening; Finishing your words; Saving answer; Thinking; Speaking; Paused. Microphone is off during saving/thinking; “saved” requires durable acknowledgement.
3. On a nonempty final recognition segment with `isFinal && speechFinal`, begin a **600 ms quiet grace**. Any local voice activity, new nonempty interim or changed final cancels/restarts it. At grace expiry flush the remaining capture frame, finish the STT attempt and drain final results for at most five seconds. Only clean completed transcription with no unresolved interim may automatically become an application turn. Empty capture, timeout, failure, limit, interrupted closure or remaining provisional words pause into editable dictation; never silently promote uncertain words into an automatically sent answer. Stop before submission leaves words unsent.
4. Save that utterance verbatim as an **unreviewed transcript**, with one application request ID allocated before capture. After durable acceptance, run the existing single-attempt adaptive interpretation. Display results/gaps and speak the authorized current reply/question once. Spoken corrections preserve inspectable original sources.
5. Before speaking, open the next bounded STT attempt against the newly acknowledged session revision/current prompt. Begin assistant audio only when capture is ready, so an interruption is heard from its start. Local voice activity immediately stops audio; preserve the captured interruption as the next utterance. If input cannot become ready, pause with readable text and explicit Resume/typing controls; do not speak over an unavailable capture path or silently reconnect after failure.
6. **Use typing** stops playback/capture and keeps available unsent words in the same composer. It never cancels an accepted application save. **Resume voice conversation** is an explicit gesture; preserve any typed prefix and the current answer ID. Dictation remains available for checking each utterance.
7. At the frozen PHQ block, stop automatic capture and present the exact stem, item and four choices. Read-aloud may read the authorized wording. Only an explicit choice populates a score; ambient speech, “all zeros” or an earlier narrative cannot. Pause likewise for guided structured corrections. After the explicit block, resume natural voice through the visible Resume control.
8. At review, pause microphone and playback. Present the existing summary, gaps, sources and correction controls. Make speech-derived names, medicines, amounts, units, dates and negations easy to inspect; ask targeted clarification when uncertain rather than treating a transcript as clinical certainty. Digest-bound **Confirm and send** remains the handoff action. Partial handoffs retain their missingness; routine voice completion is never clinical clearance.

## Small engineering contract

- Add `unreviewed-transcript` to `AdaptiveTurnInput`, `TurnInput` and `SourceTurn.channel`, API validators and durable source serialization/projection. Natural voice uses it; reviewed dictation uses `confirmed-transcript`. PHQ rejects **both** speech channels for its scored items. Show the distinction in patient history, source inspection and staff handoff. Include source channel in the confirmed snapshot/digest.
- Keep raw recognized source text immutable. Final confirmation means the patient reviewed the displayed facts; it must not relabel every original transcript as verbatim verified speech. Explicitly reviewed/edited dictation or corrections create a new reviewed source through existing answer/correction paths; preserve the original and its provenance.
- Use the existing application save, interpretation, correction, review and confirmation endpoints. No new service or inference call is needed. Add an opt-in conversation controller above the existing dictation reducer: only this active mode interprets final-plus-grace as turn submission. Dictation's existing final events remain non-submitting.
- Each utterance owns one stable answer UUID and frozen save payload. STT and TTS have separate fresh attempt UUIDs. A lost save response pauses the loop: no new utterance, inference replay or audio replay. Existing explicit same-ID save/status reconciliation resolves it. Accepted interpretation remains one claimed attempt; uncertain inference uses status/discard recovery. Displayed revision, prompt, operation and playback generation reject all late results.
- Automatically opening the next STT attempt is continuation of a healthy, consented conversation, never retry of a failed/unknown attempt. Preserve current admission, one-active-capture, byte/frame/backpressure, per-attempt 60-second maximum and five-second drain limits. At capture limit, or 15 seconds without local speech after the question audio finishes, pause rather than opening more empty attempts. Quota denial pauses with typing available. No audio survives stop, backgrounding or navigation for later replay.
- Maintain actual-rate resampling and echo cancellation. Local VAD must interrupt playback independently of provider events. Speakers, headphones and Bluetooth require echo tests: assistant audio must not become a patient answer. If echo cannot be controlled on a device, use the explicit dictation fallback rather than claiming hands-free readiness. Use existing incremental MediaSource output where supported, with the documented bounded Blob fallback elsewhere.

## Acceptance and evidence

| Validation | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Offline event/clock harness | Grace cancellation/resumption, duplicate/out-of-order finals, final drain, unresolved interim, empty/limited capture, Stop at every transition; exactly one durable answer ID and one inference claim; no repeated provider attempts after ambiguity; late revision/prompt rejection; PHQ rejection for both speech channels; review/source labels and unchanged final confirmation semantics. |
| Synthetic browser/native fixture harness | Inject known PCM into the actual browser capture graph without a microphone grant; exercise resampling → same-origin WS → application acknowledgement → interpretation → streamed audio, interruption, same-response autoplay recovery, reconnect denial, quota failure, typing switch and summary. Verify stored unreviewed channel/text, provenance and no duplicate saves. Fixture sound does not establish real microphone behavior. |
| Human desktop + mobile microphone | At least **20 successful representative turns per device class**, plus denial/revocation, background/lock, network loss, Bluetooth/speaker/headphone changes and all recovery paths. Cover medical names, units, numbers, negation, correction and interruption; check real echo and transcript accuracy. |
| Timing | Local visible feedback within **1 second**; user Stop or local speech onset to audible silence **under 500 ms**; p95 physical end-of-utterance to usable transcript and separately to first audible relevant reply **at most 5 seconds**. Include endpoint/grace, drain, durable save, model, next-capture readiness and browser decode in the latter. At **8 seconds**, show still-working plus Stop/typing without another request. Record MediaSource versus Blob fallback separately; provider first-byte time alone is not browser latency. |

Coordinator-supplied native evidence at drafting: synthetic TTS first bytes 327 ms / complete body 470 ms; a streamed 12.65-second STT fixture matched its expected wording. These are useful component observations, not this contract's natural conversation or human-device acceptance. No provider calls or browser permissions were used to write this document.
